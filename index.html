To My Bibilabs

Happy anniversary labilabs q. I just want to say thank you for staying with me and for being part of my life. I'm so lucky to have you, and I'm really thankful na ikaw yung kasama ko sa journey na ito.

Thank you for your love, care, patience, and understanding. Thank you for making me happy even in the simplest ways. You make my days better just by being there. Hindi mo man alam, pero malaking bagay ka sa buhay ko. You give me reasons to smile, and you make me feel loved in ways I can't always explain.

And of course, thank you for putting up with me. I know minsan ang kulit ko, minsan and sometimes I can be a little annoying. Pero sorry na neng. You can't return me anymore, no refunds, no exchanges. Kidding aside, I hope you know that I'll always do my best to make you happy, even if my jokes are sometimes the reason why you get annoyed.

Sorry rin sa mga times na napapairal ko pride ko, and sa mga bagay na hindi ko nagagawa nang tama. I know I'm not a perfect boyfriend, but I promise to keep learning and improving for us. I want to love you better every day and make you feel that you are always important to me. I hope we continue to grow together, support each other, and achieve our dreams side by side. Sana kahit dumating yung mga difficult days, piliin pa rin natin ang isa't isa. I want more memories with you, more laughs, more hugs, more dates, more random pictures, more food trips, and more anniversaries together.

And please, don't ever get tired of me, okay? Kasi ako, hindi ako magsasawang kulitin ka, lambingin ka, and remind you how beautiful you are. Kahit ilang beses mo pa akong sabihan na ang kulit ko, I'll still find a way to make you smile. Always remember na mahal na mahal kita. I'm proud of us and everything we've been through. Thank you for being a part of my life, my happiness, my favorite person, and my forever kakulitan.

Happy anniversary baby! Here's to more months and years together. More happiness, more adventures, more laughter, and of course, more little arguments about who's right (kahit alam naman nating ikaw lagi ang panalo) I love you so much, and I will always be thankful that I have you.

I love you, i miss you so much!

Jungook/Baby mu
